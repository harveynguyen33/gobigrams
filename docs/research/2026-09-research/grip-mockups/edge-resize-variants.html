Your words: “ngoài 4 góc chéo kéo co giãn, thì các góc còn lại khi hover để hiện icon kéo thì hơi khó.” I measured it in the live app. There are two causes, not one.

So the mid-edge grip is a 1px line underneath a 9px connect dot. That is why it feels unfindable.

A — wide edge band

Each edge gets a 10px invisible band with a 1px visible line in it. Grip becomes as easy as the corners. Connect dots stay on top (z=5 vs 4), so drawing a wire is unchanged — the band is only reachable beside the dot.

My take: A. It fixes the actual complaint (a 1px target) with a CSS-only change, keeps single-axis resize, and — the part that matters — leaves the connect dot winning its own spot, so drawing wires is untouched. C is A plus visible ticks; nicer to look at, slightly busier. B is the simplest but throws away single-axis resizing, which you use.

One caveat I will check after you pick: the app enforces “exactly ONE selection-coloured line” on a selected node (harness package-o-selection-stack.mjs) — that is the rule that killed the old “2 borders”. A’s 1px in-band line must not become a second one; the band itself stays borderless, so it should be safe, and I will verify it rather than assume.
