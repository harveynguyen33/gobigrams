Reshape an edge — drag its middle to move the line

Your words: “kéo 1 connect dài… bạn thường sẽ có 1 vài dot ở giữa để kéo cái connection line đó ra vị trí khác cho đẹp → chứ không phải là đổi connect tới shape khác.” So: a handle at the MIDDLE that bends the line — not a handle at the ends that re-attaches it.

Measured first — the two features are different things

Today's 4 big dots (r=14) sit exactly ON the edge endpoints — measured at the source node's right border and the target's left border. That is Round Q's reconnect: it re-attaches an end to a different node.
What you want a handle at the midpoint that reshapes the path. The two ends never move.
React Flow support getSmoothStepPath takes centerX/centerY (supported) — but getBezierPath and getStraightPath take nothing.
Consequence a mid-drag handle is native for step / smoothstep and needs a custom path for bezier. This decides which variants are cheap.

My take: A. It is exactly what you described — one dot mid-line, drag it, the connection moves for looks, the ends never change. It uses React Flow's own centerX/centerY, so it is native for the step and smoothstep edges you already use.

B is the 80% answer: most of the time the only reason you want to move a line is that two parallel edges overlap, and up/down fixes that with half the code. C is the most powerful and the most work (custom path maths for bezier, plus persistence). All three keep the Round Q end dots — this is a new handle, not a replacement.

One thing I will verify rather than assume: the mid-drag handle must not fight the edge label (which already sits at the path's centre) or the click that selects the edge. I will measure the overlap and report the numbers before shipping.
