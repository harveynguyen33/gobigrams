Resize grip — 3 variants

Today's grip: 10×10 solid square, no rail, appearing the instant a node is selected. Below: three lighter redesigns. In all three the grip also appears when you touch the wire (hover the line on the left of each node) — that is the new part.

Hover a node's WIRE → the grip set fades in. Click the node → it stays. Click “grip: always” / “on wire touch” to compare reveal rules.

Variant A — hairline

A 1px rail traces the box; 7px squares only at the corners. Quietest possible affordance — reads as “this is selected”, not “grab me”.

My take: B is the one that also fixes a real complaint you had — “khó kéo thấy” (hard to tell what you’re grabbing). The corner brackets tell you exactly which of the four corners is under your pointer, and edge ticks show the one-axis resizes. A if you want the canvas as quiet as possible; C if you want it to feel designed. All three keep the invisible 22px hit area, so none of them is harder to grab than today.
