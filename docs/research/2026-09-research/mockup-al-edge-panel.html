AL-1 — how a SELECTED line should look (the ugly one)

Today the selected edge gets a +5px dashed halo around its body (strokeWidth+5, dasharray 6 4). That is the fat dashed outline — and it fights the flow animation, which is why it looks worst there. All four below are drawn with flow animation running, on an edge whose authored colour is amber.

A — glowing twin

A 1px solid path in the selection blue drawn EXACTLY on the body, plus a soft glow. The line reads as "lit up", not "outlined". No dash.
Authored colour stays fully visible. Closest to today, minus the dash.

B — endpoint-only

The body is left completely alone. Only the two endpoints light up in the selection colour. Never touches the line, so animation is untouched.
Cleanest, weakest signal on a long edge.

C — old look + truthful core recommended

The body turns selection-blue exactly as it did before ROUND AK (the look he liked), and the authored colour is preserved as a thin core line INSIDE it. So it looks like the old behaviour but never lies about the colour.
The body is heavier while selected, which is exactly what the old look did.

D — revert (accept the lie)

Put the old !important back: the body is forced to the selection colour and the authored colour is invisible until you deselect. This is the bug, restored on purpose.
He named this fallback himself.

AL-3 — label background at None, and the two new colour pickers

He wants None as the default, the text always above the line, and — because a bare label gets muddled with the line — the text to blend with the canvas. The answer is a canvas-coloured halo behind the glyphs (tldraw's own technique), not a box.

The two behaviour questions (no mockup needed — just say which)

Q1 · label drag rails

Today two rails move the label for you: snap-to-centre (inside 10 screen px it jumps to centre) and clamp (it slides back so the box clears the arrowheads). Both together are what makes a far-dragged label hop back when he clicks it.
a) remove BOTH — anywhere, no help, Reset is the only way back (his words)
b) remove the clamp, KEEP snap-to-centre so centring is easy without Reset
c) remove both AND rename Reset → Center label

Q2 · flow animation, after the button system lands

Five options (None/Dots/Dash/Pulse/Icon) plus Count+Speed+glyph. With the unified system this becomes two tidy rows.
a) keep all five
b) drop Pulse too — he found Envelope redundant, and Pulse is the other one that reads as a generic "something moves"
c) keep five but hide Count/Speed unless the choice actually uses them
