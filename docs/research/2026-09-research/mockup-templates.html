E · What this round does NOT do
Not in scopeWhy
Insert a template into the CURRENT board A template BELONGS to a preset. Seeding a C4 template into a Mixed board would give it C4 nodes under Mixed's edge rules and no C4 legend. You settled this: template = new board only.
A level FILTER control 7 cards, no level with more than 2 rows — a filter would hide nothing and cost a row of chrome. You settled this: label first.
Reinstating the “Diagram type” picker It was removed on purpose earlier; preset survives as data driving 7 behaviours. The template list is now the discovery surface instead.
Changing the layout engine See tab 3, note on lanes — auto-arrange already leaves lane bands behind. Separate round.
A toolbar entry for the gallery Cheap to add later with no data change; the phone toolbar is crowded. Open question for you.

The three levels are NOT “5, 10 and 16 shapes picked by feel”. They are three contiguous bands of the flowchart palette you already curated in order — which makes the claim measurable: level 1 = palette rows 0–4, level 2 = rows 0–9, level 3 = rows 0–15.

① Cơ bản · BASIC BASIC 10 nodes · 11 edges · 5 shapes · 1 loop
Shapes: terminator, process, decision, io, document — the five that appear in almost every real process sketch, and the first five rows of the panel, so a user who has only used Basic has already met every shape in it. No lanes, no note: nothing needs explaining.
② Nâng cao · ADVANCED ADVANCED ~17 nodes · 10 shapes · 2 lanes
Adds subprocess, preparation, manual, delay, connector — each answers a question a beginner rarely asks (defined elsewhere / setup / by hand / wait / jump), and each sits below the fold in the panel. Lanes arrive here.
③ Phức tạp · COMPLEX — full notation COMPLEX 31 nodes · 27 edges · ALL 16 shapes · 3 loops · 2 pages
Every one of the 16 ISO 5807 symbols has an honest job, not a metaphor — the two storage symbols are the giveaway: storeddata = the wash log kept between washes; internalstorage = the drum holding items right now and gone when the run ends. offpage = the procedure is documented on two pages.
The 16 symbols, and how far down the palette each level goes

You said fun / everyday is fine — so here are four candidate scenarios, each drawn as a mini flowchart so you can judge by eye. Score = how many of the 16 symbols get an honest job (not a bolted-on one). Pick one; the rest are dropped.

⚠ First, the lane problem — measured, and it decides one thing in this spec
WhatMeasured result
Auto-arrange on a template WITH lanes The engine places 11 of 14 nodes; the 3 lane bands are FROZEN at their authored y (0 / 70 / 320). The laid-out flow runs y 0 → 780 while the lanes only cover 0 → 550. Result: only 6 of 10 flow nodes stay inside a lane band — the rest spill out of the swimlane entirely.
On a 31-node / 3-lane template Far worse. Pressing Auto-arrange on the Complex template would visibly scatter the flow out of its bands.
Recommended action Do not use Lane nodes in the three new templates. Use plain label nodes for phase headings (they are canvas annotations and are never laid out). Zero layout-engine risk, the template ships clean, and the existing approval template is untouched. A separate round can make lanes layout-aware and then the templates adopt them as a pure data edit.
A · Wash day current pick everyday · 16/16 honest
For: understood with zero training, no brand/locale, long enough to have real phases. The giveaway pair: storeddata = the wash log kept between washes vs internalstorage = the drum holding items only during the run. Against: a household chore for a tool people use at work.
B · Flat-pack furniture FUNNY 16/16 honest
For: funnier and equally universal; the symbol fit is arguably tighter than wash day — offpage is literally page 2 of the instructions, storeddata is the bag of leftover screws you keep forever, internalstorage is the parts held in the box during assembly, display is the step diagram you keep re-reading, and delay is waiting for the glue. Against: a long flow needs many steps to stay honest (allen key, two-person step, wrong-hole redo).
C · Getting the cat into the carrier FUNNY 13/16 honest
For: the funniest, and the loops are earned rather than decorative (cat escapes → back to step 1, honestly). Against: storeddata and internalstorage and offpage are contrived — you would be faking 3 of 16. Verdict: reject for the Complex level, excellent as a small single-level example later.
D · Out the door on time everyday 14/16 honest
For: the most universally relatable morning; real waits (delay), real preparation, real manual steps. Against: offpage and storeddata are a stretch — 2 of 16 faked. Verdict: good, second to A/B.
